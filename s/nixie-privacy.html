Nixie Clock Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Nixie Clock does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your clock preferences, including time format, time zone, visual settings, and timer duration, are stored locally on your Apple Vision Pro. Nixie Clock does not operate an account system or server that receives these settings.

Purchases

Nixie Clock Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Nixie Clock checks the entitlement supplied by StoreKit to determine whether Pro features are unlocked. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Nixie Clock contains no third-party analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking technology.

Children's privacy

Because Nixie Clock does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.
