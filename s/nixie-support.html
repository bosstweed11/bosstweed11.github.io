Nixie Clock Support

Help with Nixie Clock and the optional Nixie Clock Pro upgrade.

Restore Nixie Clock Pro

Open Nixie Clock Settings using the gear ornament, choose Unlock Nixie Clock Pro, then select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Pro features are unavailable

Confirm that Apple Vision Pro is connected to the internet, then use Restore Purchases. If the product is temporarily unavailable, wait a moment and select Retry Store Connection.

Contact

For support or feedback, email me@andrew.beer. Including your Nixie Clock version and visionOS version will help with troubleshooting.
